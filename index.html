Frequently Asked Questions

What is VPS hosting?

VPS (Virtual Private Server) hosting provides dedicated resources in a virtualized environment. You get full control over your server with root access, guaranteed CPU, RAM, and storage resources.

Which VPS plan should I choose?

Choose based on your needs: Starter for small projects, Growth for growing businesses, Scale for high-traffic applications, and Dedicated Core for resource-intensive workloads.

Can I upgrade my VPS later?

Yes, you can upgrade your VPS plan as your needs grow. Contact our sales team to discuss upgrade options and ensure a smooth transition.

Is root access included?

Yes, all our VPS plans include full root access, giving you complete control over your server environment and software installations.

How does annual billing work?

With annual billing, you pay for 10 months and get 12 months of service. This gives you significant savings compared to monthly billing.
